REF#GOI-KISAN-01
CENTRAL GOVT

PM-KISAN (Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi)

Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, GoI

DIRECT INCOME ASSISTANCE
₹6,000 / Year
Paid in 3 equal 4-monthly installments of ₹2,000 directly via Aadhaar DBT
CRITICAL ELIGIBILITY RULES
  • [✔]All landholder farmer families with cultivable landholding in their name
  • [✔]Excludes institutional landholders, constitutional post holders & income tax payers
  • [✔]Mandatory e-KYC via biometric or Aadhaar OTP authentication
REQUIRED DOCUMENTS:
Pahani / RTCAadhaar CardNPCI-Active Bank A/c
Official Portal (pmkisan.gov.in) north_east
REF#KA-GANGA-02
KARNATAKA STATE

Ganga Kalyana Scheme

Social Welfare & Backward Classes Dept, Karnataka

COMPLETE IRRIGATION UNIT
100% Free Borewell
Full financial grant up to ₹3.75 Lakhs for drilling, pump set & ESCOM energization
CRITICAL ELIGIBILITY RULES
  • [✔]Small & marginal farmers belonging to SC, ST, OBC & Minorities
  • [✔]Annual family income limit ≤ ₹1,50,000 (Rural) / ₹2,00,000 (Urban)
  • [✔]Land holding between 1.5 to 5.0 acres in dryland/rainfed areas
REQUIRED DOCUMENTS:
RTC / PahaniCaste & Income CertAadhaarLand Map
Official Portal (kppp.karnataka.gov.in) north_east
REF#GOI-PMFBY-03
COMPREHENSIVE COVER

PMFBY (Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana)

MoA&FW & Samrakshane Karnataka

CROP LOSS RISK COVER
1.5% - 2.0% Premium
Government pays the rest; covers drought, floods, hailstorms & pest attacks
CRITICAL ELIGIBILITY RULES
  • [✔]Farmers cultivating notified crops (Paddy, Ragi, Togari, Cotton, Maize, etc.)
  • [✔]Both loanee and non-loanee farmers eligible across notified hoblis
  • [✔]Post-harvest loss coverage up to 14 days for crops drying on field
REQUIRED DOCUMENTS:
Pahani / RTCSowing CertificateBank Passbook
Official Portal (samrakshane.karnataka.gov.in) north_east
REF#GOI-KUSUM-04
SOLAR ENERGY

PM-KUSUM (Solar Agricultural Pumpsets)

Ministry of New & Renewable Energy & KREDL Karnataka

CLEAN SOLAR IRRIGATION
Up to 90% Subsidy
30% Central + up to 60% Karnataka state subsidy on 3 to 7.5 HP standalone solar pumps
CRITICAL ELIGIBILITY RULES
  • [✔]Landholders with confirmed ground/open water source without grid connection
  • [✔]Eliminates dependency on diesel pumps and erratic night power supplies
  • [✔]Priority for farmers utilizing micro-irrigation systems
REQUIRED DOCUMENTS:
RTC (Pahani)Water Source CertAadhaar CardNOC
Official Portal (souramitra.com) north_east
REF#GOI-PMKSY-05
WATER EFFICIENCY

PMKSY (Per Drop More Crop — Drip & Sprinkler)

Department of Agriculture & Horticulture, Karnataka

MICRO-IRRIGATION SUBSIDY
70% to 90% Subsidy
90% subsidy for small/marginal farmers (≤ 5 acres); 70% for other farmers (≤ 12.5 acres)
CRITICAL ELIGIBILITY RULES
  • [✔]Cultivable landholding between 0.5 and 12.5 acres
  • [✔]Functional water source (borewell, open well, or lift pipe)
  • [✔]Must not have availed drip subsidy for the same survey number in 3 years
REQUIRED DOCUMENTS:
Pahani / RTCWater Source ProofSmall Farmer Cert
Official Portal (pmksy.gov.in) north_east
