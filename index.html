Government Farmer Schemes

Verified central and Karnataka state welfare programs, direct income transfers (DBT), solar subsidies, and crop risk shields.

REF#GOI-KISAN-01 Central Govt
payments

PM-KISAN (Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi)

Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, GoI

Direct Income Assistance
₹6,000 / Year
Paid in 3 equal 4-monthly installments of ₹2,000 directly via Aadhaar DBT
Critical Eligibility Rules
  • check_circleAll landholder farmer families with cultivable landholding in their name
  • check_circleExcludes institutional landholders, constitutional post holders & income tax payers
  • check_circleMandatory e-KYC via biometric or Aadhaar OTP authentication
Required Documents:
Pahani / RTC Aadhaar Card NPCI-Active Bank A/c
Official Portal (pmkisan.gov.in) north_east
REF#KA-GANGA-02 Karnataka State
water_drop

Ganga Kalyana Scheme

Social Welfare & Backward Classes Dept, Karnataka

Complete Irrigation Unit
100% Free Borewell
Full financial grant up to ₹3.75 Lakhs for drilling, pump set & ESCOM energization
Critical Eligibility Rules
  • check_circleSmall & marginal farmers belonging to SC, ST, OBC & Minorities
  • check_circleAnnual family income limit ≤ ₹1,50,000 (Rural) / ₹2,00,000 (Urban)
  • check_circleLand holding between 1.5 to 5.0 acres in dryland/rainfed areas
Required Documents:
RTC / Pahani Caste & Income Cert Aadhaar Land Map
Official Portal (kppp.karnataka.gov.in) north_east
REF#GOI-PMFBY-03 Comprehensive Cover
shield

PMFBY (Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana)

MoA&FW & Samrakshane Karnataka

Crop Loss Risk Cover
1.5% - 2.0% Premium
Government pays the rest; covers drought, floods, hailstorms & pest attacks
Critical Eligibility Rules
  • check_circleFarmers cultivating notified crops (Paddy, Ragi, Togari, Cotton, Maize, etc.)
  • check_circleBoth loanee and non-loanee farmers eligible across notified hoblis
  • check_circlePost-harvest loss coverage up to 14 days for crops drying on field
Required Documents:
Pahani / RTC Sowing Certificate Bank Passbook
Official Portal (samrakshane.karnataka.gov.in) north_east
REF#GOI-KUSUM-04 Solar Energy
solar_power

PM-KUSUM (Solar Agricultural Pumpsets)

Ministry of New & Renewable Energy & KREDL Karnataka

Clean Solar Irrigation
Up to 90% Subsidy
30% Central + up to 60% Karnataka state subsidy on 3 to 7.5 HP standalone solar pumps
Critical Eligibility Rules
  • check_circleLandholders with confirmed ground/open water source without grid connection
  • check_circleEliminates dependency on diesel pumps and erratic night power supplies
  • check_circlePriority for farmers utilizing micro-irrigation systems
Required Documents:
RTC (Pahani) Water Source Cert Aadhaar Card NOC
Official Portal (souramitra.com) north_east
REF#GOI-PMKSY-05 Water Efficiency
waves

PMKSY (Per Drop More Crop — Drip & Sprinkler)

Department of Agriculture & Horticulture, Karnataka

Micro-Irrigation Subsidy
70% to 90% Subsidy
90% subsidy for small/marginal farmers (≤ 5 acres); 70% for other farmers (≤ 12.5 acres)
Critical Eligibility Rules
  • check_circleCultivable landholding between 0.5 and 12.5 acres
  • check_circleFunctional water source (borewell, open well, or lift pipe)
  • check_circleMust not have availed drip subsidy for the same survey number in 3 years
Required Documents:
Pahani / RTC Water Source Proof Small Farmer Cert
Official Portal (pmksy.gov.in) north_east
REF#RBI-KCC-06 Farm Credit

Kisan Credit Card (KCC) & Interest Subvention

Reserve Bank of India & NABARD

Concessional Loan Facility
4.0% Effective Interest
Credit limit up to ₹3,00,000; base 7% interest minus 3% prompt repayment incentive
Critical Eligibility Rules
  • check_circleAll farmers, owner cultivators, tenant farmers, oral lessees & sharecroppers
  • check_circleCollateral-free agricultural loans up to ₹1,60,000
  • check_circleSimple revolving credit card valid for 5 years with annual review
Required Documents:
Pahani / RTC Aadhaar Card PAN Card Crop Declaration
Official Portal (myscheme.gov.in) north_east

Karnataka Krishi Bhagya Scheme

Department of Agriculture, Govt of Karnataka

Rainwater Harvesting
80% - 90% Subsidy
Financial assistance for farm pond (Krishi Honda) excavation, polythene lining & pump
Critical Eligibility Rules
  • check_circleRainfed/dryland farmers across 100+ notified dry taluks in 25 districts
  • check_circlePromotes on-farm water security to sustain crops through dry spells
  • check_circlePackages include farm ponds, pump sets, and shade net polyhouses
Required Documents:
Pahani / RTC Aadhaar Card Bank Passbook RSK Field Form
Official Portal (raitamitra.karnataka.gov.in) north_east
REF#KA-SIRI-08 Millet Mission
eco

Raitha Siri Scheme (Millet Promotion)

Department of Agriculture, Govt of Karnataka

Millet Cultivation Incentive
₹10,000 / Hectare
Direct financial incentive to farmers growing climate-smart Siridhanya millets
Critical Eligibility Rules
  • check_circleFarmers cultivating certified millets: Ragi, Jowar, Bajra, Foxtail, Kodo & Little millet
  • check_circleCrops verified via the official Karnataka Crop Survey (Bele Darshak) mobile app
  • check_circleDirect DBT credit into the farmer's registered bank account
Required Documents:
Pahani / RTC (Millet entry) FRUITS ID Aadhaar Bank A/c
Official Portal (fruits.karnataka.gov.in) north_east
