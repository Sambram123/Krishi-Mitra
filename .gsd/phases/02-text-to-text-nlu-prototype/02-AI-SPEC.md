## 1. System Classification

**System Type:** Hybrid — Structured Extraction (primary) + Conversational dialogue management (secondary)

**Description:**
The Phase 2 NLU layer turns rural Kannada (and code-mixed Kannada–English) farmer utterances into a
validated `FarmerAgriculturalProfile` JSON object, merges it into per-session conversation state, and
detects which mandatory slots are still missing. The extracted profile is handed to the deterministic
rule engine; the LLM then phrases a short, respectful Kannada follow-up or result summary from rules
the engine already decided. Users are small and marginal farmers in Karnataka, often low-literacy,
speaking North Karnataka (*-aitri*), Old Mysore (*-kanri*), and code-mixed dialects, sometimes via an
intermediary at a Grama One centre. "Good" looks like: the correct slots are captured on the first
turn, dialect terms and land units are normalized correctly, no value is invented, missing slots are
asked for one at a time in warm Kannada, and every spoken/written claim is traceable to the rule engine.

**Critical Failure Modes:**
1. **Eligibility hallucination** — the NLU or reply layer asserts eligibility, a benefit amount, or an
   exclusion outcome that the rule engine did not produce (violates ADR-001).
2. **Silent slot corruption** — wrong land-unit conversion (gunta → acre, hectare → acre), wrong crop
   canonicalization (Togari → Pigeon Pea, Jola → Sorghum), or an invented/rounded land size, causing a
   wrong eligibility result downstream.
3. **Phantom or missing slots** — asserting a slot with no textual evidence, or failing to detect a
   genuinely missing mandatory slot, so the conversation either loops or proceeds with an incomplete profile.
4. **Dialect/code-mix misparse** — low recall on North Karnataka and Old Mysore forms and English
   loanwords the farmer actually uses (borewell, subsidy, loan, drip), forcing repetitive re-asks.
5. **Transparency violation** — a synthetic/demo scheme is presented as a real government entitlement
   without the `[DEMO / TEST CASE]` marker, or a real scheme is described with fabricated rules.

## 1b. Domain Context

> Researched by `gsd-domain-researcher`. Grounds the evaluation strategy in domain expert knowledge.

**Industry Vertical:** AgriTech / public-sector agricultural extension and government scheme advisory (Karnataka, India)

**User Population:** Small and marginal farmers (≤ 5 acres) in rural Karnataka; tenant and joint-family
farmers; low digital literacy; Kannada-first with English loanwords; often assisted by a Grama One /
Raitha Samparka Kendra (RSK) operator. Conversations happen on shared/borrowed devices and intermittent
connectivity.

**Stakes Level:** High — not life-critical, but wrong advice causes missed entitlements, wasted travel to
government offices, agent fees, or false expectations about money.

**Output Consequence:** The farmer acts on the extracted profile and the resulting scheme list. A wrong
profile silently produces a wrong eligibility outcome; a confident-but-wrong sentence can send a farmer
to the wrong office or make them believe they will receive money they are not entitled to.

### What Domain Experts Evaluate Against

| Dimension | Good (expert accepts) | Bad (expert flags) | Stakes | Source |
|-----------|----------------------|--------------------|--------|--------|
| Eligibility fidelity | Output matches the scheme's official criteria and exclusions exactly | Plausible but wrong eligibility; invented criteria | Critical | `schemes_knowledge_base.md`, ADR-001 |
| Dialect comprehension | Maps *-aitri* / *-kanri*, *Togari*, *Kushki*, *Gunta*, and code-mixed terms to canonical slots | Treats "gunta" as acre; misses loanwords; asks again for what was said | High | `voice_interaction_design.md`, `schemes_knowledge_base.md` §2 |
| Land-unit normalization | Gunta × 0.025, hectare × 2.471, exact acre values preserved | Rounds 2.5 acres to 3; unit silently dropped | Critical | `requirements.md` §1.2 |
| Missing-slot handling | Asks only for genuinely missing mandatory slots, one at a time, in context | Re-asks answered slots; skips land size; interrogates in a loop | High | `voice_interaction_design.md` §2 |
| Crop canonicalization | Togari→Pigeon Pea/Red Gram, Jola→Sorghum/Jowar, Ragi→Finger Millet, Kabbu→Sugarcane | Leaves local name only, or maps to the wrong standard crop | Critical | `schemes_knowledge_base.md` §2 |
| Farmer-safe phrasing | Warm, respectful (*ನೀವು*, *-ರೆ*), ≤ 25 words, no false promises | Legalistic jargon, over-promising amounts, blaming the farmer | High | `voice_interaction_design.md` §1, ADR-002 |
| Document accuracy | Correct documents incl. NPCI-seeded account, and where to obtain them (Grama One/RSK/Seva Sindhu) | Omits a mandatory document; invents an office | High | `schemes_knowledge_base.md`, `requirements.md` §4 |
| Transparency | Real vs synthetic clearly marked; audit reasons shown | Synthetic scheme shown as a real entitlement | Critical | `prd.md` §2, `dataset_research.md` §1 |
| Ownership nuance | Tenant farmers routed away from owner-only DBT (PM-KISAN) and toward tenant-eligible paths (PMFBY via tenancy certificate) | Tenant treated as owner; outright "not eligible, goodbye" | High | `requirements.md` §4 |

### Known Failure Modes in This Domain

- **Unit confusion:** *Gunta* (1/40 acre = 0.025 acre) vs acre vs hectare; farmers frequently give mixed units ("two and a half acres", "40 guntas").
- **Crop synonym trap:** *Togari* = Pigeon Pea = Red Gram; *Jola* = Jowar = Sorghum; *Ragi* = Finger Millet; *Kabbu* = Sugarcane. A single mismatch flips PMFBY eligibility.
- **Tenant vs owner blindness:** PM-KISAN requires land ownership in the family's name; tenants are excluded from owner-only DBT and must be redirected, not stonewalled.
- **Season / notified-area mismatch:** PMFBY applies only to notified crops, seasons, and notified taluks; assuming coverage is a real-world harm.
- **Over-promising benefit amounts:** PM-KISAN installments/arrears and PMFBY payouts depend on external state; the assistant must not compute or promise a payout.
- **Exclusion blindness:** income-tax payees, constitutional-post holders, serving/retired government employees, and institutional landholders are PM-KISAN exclusions.
- **Synthetic-scheme leakage:** demo schemes are always one careless sentence away from sounding like a real entitlement.
- **Aadhaar-NPCI assumption:** assuming the farmer's bank account is Aadhaar-seeded when it may not be.

### Regulatory / Compliance Context

- **DPDP Act 2023 (India):** farmer data (land records, Aadhaar references, bank status, location) requires a lawful purpose, notice/consent, purpose limitation, and data minimization. Collect only what a scheme rule needs; do not persist Aadhaar or bank account numbers.
- **Aadhaar Act constraints:** do not store or display full Aadhaar numbers; refer to "Aadhaar" as a document to bring, never as a stored identifier. Redact 12-digit patterns from logs/traces.
- **Advisory disclaimer:** the assistant is a prototype/decision-support tool, not a government authority; state that final eligibility is determined by the department/portal.
- **Synthetic-scheme disclosure:** any fictional scheme must carry a clear demo/test-case marker in both text and (later) audio.
- **No financial advice:** do not state loan/insurance payout amounts as guarantees.

### Domain Expert Roles for Evaluation

| Role | Responsibility |
|------|---------------|
| Karnataka Agriculture Dept. extension officer / RSK staff | Rule and exclusion calibration; labels eligibility ground truth |
| Grama One / Seva Sindhu operator | Validates document lists, application channels, and "how to obtain" text |
| Kannada linguist / native dialect speaker (North Karnataka + Old Mysore) | Labels dialect transcripts; reviews canonicalization mappings |
| Farmer representative / agri NGO field worker | Reviews phrasing, comprehension, and respectfulness with real users |
| Legal / policy reviewer | Reviews disclaimers, DPDP consent copy, and Aadhaar handling |

## 2. Framework Decision

**Selected Framework:** Google Gen AI SDK (`google-genai`, the official Gemini Python SDK) + Pydantic v2 for schema-constrained output, with a thin provider adapter for an Ollama local fallback.

**Version:** `google-genai>=1.0,<2.0` (pin exact at implementation time), `pydantic>=2.6,<3`, `python-dotenv>=1.0`. Local fallback: Ollama `qwen2.5:7b-instruct` (primary fallback) / `llama3.2:3b` (low-RAM fallback).

**Rationale:**
Phase 2 performs exactly one schema-constrained extraction call (plus one short phrasing call) per
turn. There is no multi-agent collaboration, no graph traversal, no retrieval index, and no
provider-agnostic portability requirement for the hackathon demo — the stack is already committed to
Gemini 2.5 Flash's free tier in `prd.md`, `architecture.md`, and `dataset_research.md`. The cheapest,
most debuggable, lowest-latency path is the provider's native SDK with native JSON-schema/Pydantic
structured output. This also matches the reference matrix's anti-pattern #1 ("using LangChain for
simple chatbots — direct SDK call is less code, faster, and easier to debug"). Conversation state is a
small Pydantic object merged in Python, so a graph/checkpointing engine would be pure overhead. The
provider boundary is isolated in `providers/` so the Ollama fallback and a future model swap stay cheap.

**Alternatives Considered:**

| Framework | Ruled Out Because |
|-----------|------------------|
| Google ADK | Built-in sessions/memory/E2E orchestration are valuable at Stage 3+, but at Stage 1 they add a framework mental model and scaffolding for a single extraction call. Revisit if Phase 4 introduces agentic tool routing. |
| LangChain | Abstraction overhead and p99 latency for what is one structured-output call; anti-pattern for a simple, well-defined pipeline. |
| LlamaIndex | RAG-first; the scheme catalog is structured JSON consumed by a deterministic evaluator, not a retrieval corpus. Retrieval quality is not the bottleneck. |
| LangGraph | Graph state + checkpointing is overkill for linear slot-filling; session state is a small dict, not a branching workflow. |
| CrewAI | Multi-agent role abstraction is unused; its weaknesses (limited checkpointing) are irrelevant and its strengths are not exercised. |
| OpenAI Agents SDK | Would force a provider swap away from the committed free Gemini tier; same simplicity already available natively. |
| the agent Agent SDK (Anthropic) | Model lock-in to a non-chosen provider; designed for code-agent tool use, not Kannada extraction. |
| Haystack | Explicit pipelines offer no advantage over plain Python here, with a smaller ecosystem. |
| AutoGen / AG2 | Ecosystem fragmentation risk; conversational multi-agent patterns are not needed. |

**Vendor Lock-In Accepted:** Partial — the demo deliberately uses Gemini's free tier for latency and
cost, but the extractor sits behind a `NLUProvider` interface with an Ollama fallback, all schemas are
ours (Pydantic), and no conversation data is persisted by the provider. No data lock-in.

### Entry Point Pattern
```python
from google import genai
from google.genai import types
from pydantic import BaseModel, Field
from typing import Literal

class LandSize(BaseModel):
    value: float = Field(description="Number exactly as spoken")
    unit: Literal["acre", "gunta", "hectare"] = Field(description="Unit exactly as spoken")
    # normalized_acres is computed in Python, NOT by the model

class ExtractOut(BaseModel):
    dialect_region: Literal["North_Karnataka", "Old_Mysore", "Coastal", "General"] | None = None
    district: str | None = None
    land_size: LandSize | None = None
    ownership_type: Literal["Owner", "Tenant", "Joint Family", "Unknown"] = "Unknown"
    crops_local: list[str] = Field(default_factory=list)
    irrigation_source: Literal["Rainfed", "Borewell", "Canal", "Open Well", "None"] | None = None
    assistance_intent: Literal[
        "scheme_discovery", "crop_loss_compensation", "solar_pump",
        "loan_waiver", "fertilizer_seed_subsidy", "general_inquiry"
    ] | None = None

client = genai.Client(api_key=os.environ["GEMINI_API_KEY"])

def extract(transcript: str, known: dict, missing: list[str]) -> ExtractOut:
    prompt = build_extraction_prompt(transcript, known, missing)  # system rules + few-shot
    resp = client.models.generate_content(
        model="gemini-2.5-flash",
        contents=prompt,
        config=types.GenerateContentConfig(
            system_instruction=SYSTEM_RULES,
            response_mime_type="application/json",
            response_schema=ExtractOut,
            temperature=0.1,
            max_output_tokens=1024,
        ),
    )
    return ExtractOut.model_validate_json(resp.text)
```

### Key Abstractions

| Concept | What It Is | When You Use It |
|---------|-----------|-----------------|
| `genai.Client` | SDK entry point holding credentials and transport | Created once at process start, reused |
| `client.models.generate_content` | Single-turn generation | Every extraction turn |
| `GenerateContentConfig.response_schema` | Constrains output to a Pydantic/JSON schema | All extraction calls (never free-text for slots) |
| `GenerateContentConfig.system_instruction` | Global role + hard rules, stored separately from user text | Policy rules, few-shot exemplars, output contract |
| `client.aio.models.generate_content` | Async variant | Inside FastAPI/CLI async loop |
| `client.chats.create` | Stateful multi-turn wrapper | Optional; we keep state ourselves for auditability |
| `types.HarmCategory` / `finish_reason` | Safety termination signals | Detect blocked/empty candidates and retry-with-fallback |

### Common Pitfalls

1. **Do not let the model compute derived fields.** `normalized_acres` must be computed in Python — the model will round or silently change units. Ask only for spoken `value` + `unit`.
2. **Schema feature limits.** `response_schema` rejects or mishandles some JSON-schema constructs (defaults, nested `$ref` recursion, `anyOf` on complex types). Keep the extraction schema shallow, explicit, and enum-based; validate after.
3. **Free-tier rate limit is 15 RPM / 1M TPM.** Parallel turns will 429. Serialize per session, add exponential backoff with jitter, and surface a latency budget (Section 4b).
4. **Indic token cost.** Kannada text costs materially more tokens than Latin text for the same meaning; budget roughly 2× characters→tokens and keep few-shots compact.
5. **Package-name confusion.** `google-genai` (new SDK) ≠ `google-generativeai` (legacy). Install and import only one; the legacy package uses a different API surface.
6. **Blocked/empty responses.** Safety filters or truncation can return `resp.text is None` or a non-`STOP` finish reason. Handle it, then fall back to the local provider rather than crashing.
7. **JSON mode does not fix semantics.** Schema validity ≠ correct slots. Every extracted value still needs an evidence check (Section 6 guardrails).
8. **UTF-8 everywhere.** Kannada script must survive prompt construction, logging, and file writes; normalize NFC before comparison.

## 4. Implementation Guidance

**Model Configuration:**
- Extraction: `gemini-2.5-flash`, `temperature=0.1`, `top_p=0.95`, `max_output_tokens=1024`, `response_mime_type="application/json"`, `response_schema=ExtractOut`.
- Reply phrasing: same model, `temperature=0.5`, `max_output_tokens=256`, free text constrained by system rules (≤ 25 words, no eligibility claims).
- Local fallback: Ollama `qwen2.5:7b-instruct`, `temperature=0.0`, JSON mode via prompt + post-validation; accept higher latency.
- Provider selection: `NLU_PROVIDER=gemini|ollama` env var, chosen at startup, with runtime failover to Ollama on repeated 429/5xx/empty responses.

**Core Pattern:**
```
utterance → extract (LLM, slots only)
          → validate against Pydantic + transcript evidence
          → normalize (gunta/hectare→acres, crop synonyms)
          → merge into ConversationState (conflict-aware)
          → compute missing_required_slots (Python)
          → rules_engine.evaluate(profile)            ← deterministic, no LLM
          → phrase reply from engine output (LLM)    ← never invents eligibility
          → return text + structured result
```
The LLM appears twice per turn (extract, phrase) and never in the decision path. If the phrasing call
fails, fall back to a template reply built from the engine output.

**Tool Use:**
No LLM tool/function calling in Phase 2. The "tools" are Python functions: `normalize_unit`,
`canonicalize_crop`, `merge_state`, `detect_missing_slots`, `rules_engine.evaluate`. Keeping them out of
the model's control is what makes the pipeline auditable.

**State Management:**
`ConversationState` is a Pydantic model kept in an in-process `dict[session_id, ConversationState]`
(Phase 2 CLI). It holds `confirmed_profile`, `inferred_slots` (low-confidence, require confirmation),
`turn_history` (last 6 turns), and `schema_version`. Merge policy: scalars are last-write-wins **only**
when the new value is evidence-backed and non-conflicting; a conflicting scalar is quarantined and
triggers a clarification question; `crops` is a set union; `None` never overwrites a confirmed value.
State is JSON-serializable for Phase 4 persistence.

**Context Window Strategy:**
Pass only the current utterance, the confirmed profile, and the unresolved slot list — not the full
transcript — to the extractor. Keep a rolling 6-turn transcript plus a one-line summary for the
phrasing call. Never inject the scheme catalog into the extraction prompt; the extractor does not need
it and it burns tokens. Few-shot exemplars live in `system_instruction` so they can be cached.

## 4b. AI Systems Best Practices

> Written by `gsd-ai-researcher`. Cross-cutting patterns every developer building AI systems needs — independent of framework choice.

### Structured Outputs with Pydantic

Define the wire schema as the *narrow* extraction shape (spoken values + units + enums), then expand it
into the domain profile in Python. Validate evidence and retry once with error feedback on failure.

```python
from pydantic import BaseModel, Field, field_validator, ValidationError
from typing import Literal

class LandSize(BaseModel):
    value: float = Field(gt=0, le=1000)
    unit: Literal["acre", "gunta", "hectare"]

class ExtractOut(BaseModel):
    district: str | None = None
    land_size: LandSize | None = None
    crops_local: list[str] = Field(default_factory=list, max_length=10)
    irrigation_source: Literal["Rainfed", "Borewell", "Canal", "Open Well", "None"] | None = None
    ownership_type: Literal["Owner", "Tenant", "Joint Family", "Unknown"] = "Unknown"
    assistance_intent: Literal[
        "scheme_discovery", "crop_loss_compensation", "solar_pump",
        "loan_waiver", "fertilizer_seed_subsidy", "general_inquiry"
    ] | None = None

    @field_validator("district")
    @classmethod
    def no_placeholder(cls, v: str | None) -> str | None:
        if v is not None and v.strip().lower() in {"", "unknown", "n/a", "none"}:
            return None
        return v.strip() if v else v

def extract_with_retry(provider, transcript: str, known: dict, missing: list[str]) -> ExtractOut:
    try:
        return provider.extract(transcript, known, missing)
    except ValidationError as err:
        # one corrective retry: feed the validation error back, then give up to a safe empty profile
        return provider.extract(transcript, known, missing, correction=str(err))
```

Rules: `extra="forbid"` on extraction models; enums instead of free strings; no derived fields;
`None` means "not stated"; a second validation failure yields an empty extraction (never a guess) and a
clarification reply.

### Async-First Design

Use `client.aio.models.generate_content` inside an `async def` per turn; the common mistake is calling
the sync client inside an async endpoint and blocking the event loop for 400–800 ms. Stream **only** the
human-facing phrasing call (token-by-token text feels responsive in the CLI). Never stream the extraction
call — `response_schema` JSON must be complete before parsing. Run extraction and any independent
normalization concurrently with `asyncio.gather` where safe.

### Prompt Engineering Discipline

- **System instruction** = role ("Krishi Mitra NLU"), hard rules, output contract, and 4–6 compact
  few-shot exemplars covering North Karnataka, Old Mysore, code-mixed English, and gunta/hectare traps.
- **User content** = current utterance + confirmed profile JSON + unresolved slots. Nothing else.
- Explicit negative rules: "Never output eligibility, benefit amounts, or documents." "Use `null` when
  a value is not clearly stated." "Copy the unit exactly as spoken; do not convert."
- Keep few-shots in the system instruction for cacheability; keep them short (≤ 2 lines each).
- The phrasing prompt receives *only* the rule-engine result object — never the raw scheme catalog —
  so it cannot freelance.

### Context Window Management

Slot-filling needs memory of the *state*, not the transcript. Send the confirmed profile + unresolved
slots + last 6 turns. Summarize older turns into one or two sentences in Kannada/English. This keeps
every turn well under ~1.5k input tokens and avoids the classic failure where a long transcript causes
the model to re-extract already-confirmed values inconsistently.

### Cost and Latency Budget

- Free tier: 15 RPM, 1M TPM → a hard ceiling of ~1 turn / 4 s sustained. Serialize per session and back
  off on 429; a demo may run 2–3 sessions concurrently, not 20.
- Per turn: ~1.2k input + ~300 output tokens (extraction) + ~600/120 (phrasing). Extraction p95 target
  ≤ 900 ms on Gemini Flash; local fallback p95 ≤ 3 s (acceptable for dev only).
- No prompt caching across users (conversations differ); cache only the static system instruction.
- Do not call a second model for normalization — Python is faster, free, and auditable.

## 5. Evaluation Strategy

### Dimensions

| Dimension | Rubric (Pass/Fail or 1-5) | Measurement Approach | Priority |
|-----------|--------------------------|---------------------|----------|
| Slot extraction accuracy | Per-field exact match (scalars) / set-F1 ≥ 0.90 (crops); scores 3 = ≥ 0.75 | Code vs. gold labels | Critical |
| Land-unit normalization | 5 = exact acre value (gunta × 0.025, ha × 2.471); 3 = correct unit, ≤ 2% error; 1 = wrong/rounded | Code-based | Critical |
| Crop canonicalization | 5 = correct standard crop for every local term; 3 = ≥ 80%; 1 = any wrong crop | Code + LLM judge | Critical |
| Missing-slot detection | Exact match of `missing_required_slots`; false re-ask and false proceed are both fails | Code-based | Critical |
| Hallucinated slots | Pass = every asserted value has transcript evidence; any unsupported value fails | LLM judge + human audit | Critical |
| Intent classification | Accuracy ≥ 0.90 on `assistance_intent`; 3 = ≥ 0.80 | Code vs. gold | High |
| Reply appropriateness | 5 = warm, respectful, ≤ 25 words, no eligibility/amount claim; 1 = jargon or over-promise | LLM judge + human | High |
| Demo transparency | Pass = every synthetic-scheme mention carries the demo marker; any miss fails | Code regex/flag on engine output | Critical |
| E2E routing fidelity | Final eligibility equals rule-engine result on the gold profile | Code-based | Critical |
| Dialect robustness | Accuracy sliced by North Karnataka / Old Mysore / code-mixed; no slice < 0.75 | Code-based slice | High |
| Turn latency | p95 extraction ≤ 900 ms (Gemini) | Code-based timer | Medium |
| Rate-limit resilience | 429s recovered by backoff/failover ≥ 99% of the time | Code-based | Medium |

### Reference Dataset

**Size:** 40 gold examples to start, stratified (below); expand from real failure modes only.

**Composition:**
- 10 North Karnataka dialect (*-aitri*) utterances, including rainfed/togari loss cases.
- 10 Old Mysore / Mandya / Hassan dialect (*-kanri*), including solar-pump and borewell cases.
- 10 code-mixed Kannada–English ("borewell ge subsidy bekittu") utterances.
- 5 adversarial unit traps (gunta vs acre vs hectare, "2.5 acre", "40 gunta").
- 5 multi-turn missing-slot conversations (crop given, land size withheld; tenant-owner ambiguity).
- Plus: PM-KISAN exclusion probes and one synthetic-scheme intent per demo scheme.
Each row: `{id, transcript_history[], gold_profile, gold_missing_slots, gold_eligibility_set, dialect_slice, notes}`.

**Labeling:**
Kannada-speaking agriculture extension officers and a native dialect linguist label independently;
20% double-labeled, disagreements adjudicated by a third expert. LLM judges are calibrated against human
labels before use (target ≥ 0.7 correlation); uncalibrated judges may only *flag*, never score.

## 6. Guardrails

### Online (Real-Time)

| Guardrail | Trigger | Intervention |
|-----------|---------|--------------|
| Eligibility firewall | NLU JSON contains any eligibility/benefit/document key | Block, strip, re-run extraction, log violation |
| Evidence check | Asserted slot has no fuzzy match in the transcript | Downgrade to `inferred`; ask confirmation before rule engine |
| Unit sanity | `acres` outside [0.01, 100] or unit/value contradiction | Flag and re-ask, do not pass to engine |
| Conflict guard | New scalar conflicts with confirmed value | Quarantine; ask a disambiguating question |
| Demo transparency | Synthetic scheme in results without marker | Inject `[DEMO / TEST CASE]` disclaimer before output |
| PII redaction | 12-digit Aadhaar-like or bank-account-like pattern | Redact before logging/tracing; never persist |
| Safety/empty-response | `finish_reason != STOP` or empty text | Retry once, then local fallback, then template reply |

### Offline (Flywheel)

| Metric | Sampling Strategy | Action on Degradation |
|--------|------------------|----------------------|
| Field-level F1 by dialect slice | Weekly over sampled turns + full golden set | > 5% drop → revise few-shots/prompt |
| Hallucinated-slot rate | All low-confidence/`inferred` slots | > 2% → block release, audit prompt |
| False re-ask / false proceed rate | Sessions with > 4 turns | > 5% → review completeness logic |
| Reply over-promise rate | Human review of 50 sampled replies/week | Any confirmed case → prompt fix + test |
| Routing mismatch | Compare engine result vs. human-corrected profile | > 5% → escalate rule/NLU boundary |

## 7. Production Monitoring

**Tracing Tool:** Arize Phoenix (local/self-hosted, OpenInference `google-genai` instrumentation). Override to Langfuse only by explicit team decision.

**Key Metrics to Track:**
- Extraction latency p50/p95 per provider.
- Per-field extraction accuracy on sampled turns (weekly).
- Hallucinated-slot and evidence-failure counts.
- Provider 429/5xx rate and failover count.
- Turns-to-complete-profile (conversation efficiency).
- Demo-marker injection count vs. synthetic-scheme result count.
- Engine-vs-NLU routing mismatch count.

**Alert Thresholds:**
- Extraction p95 > 1.5 s → page/investigate.
- 429 rate > 5% of requests → throttle or fail over to Ollama.
- Pydantic validation failure > 3% → inspect schema/prompt drift.
- Routing mismatch > 5% on sampled turns → freeze prompt changes and audit.

**Smart Sampling Strategy:**
Prioritize for human review: low-confidence/`inferred` slots, validation retries, conflicting values,
sessions longer than 4 turns, farmer pushback ("wrong"/"not eligible"), every interaction mentioning a
synthetic scheme, and every PII-redaction event. Weekly batch of 50 sampled turns; monthly rubric
recalibration with domain experts.
